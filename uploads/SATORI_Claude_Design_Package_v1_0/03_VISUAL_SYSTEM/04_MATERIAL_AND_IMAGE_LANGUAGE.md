# Material and Image Language

## Materials

- travertine,
- hinoki,
- walnut,
- basalt,
- limestone,
- linen,
- ceramic,
- bronze,
- water,
- frameless glass,
- bamboo,
- moss.

## Image behavior

- cinematic crop,
- strong negative space,
- human scale,
- restrained depth of field,
- authentic skin and material texture,
- realistic reflection,
- soft atmospheric layers.

## Avoid

- glossy plastic surfaces,
- fake marble,
- exaggerated HDR,
- oversaturated greenery,
- perfect stock-model smiles,
- unrealistic architecture,
- empty AI-render perfection,
- and repeated near-identical images.
