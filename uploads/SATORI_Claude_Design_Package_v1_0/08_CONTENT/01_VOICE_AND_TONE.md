# Voice and Tone

## Voice

Quiet, precise, sensory, warm, restrained.

## Sentence style

Short to medium length. Deliberate. No exaggerated claims.

## Preferred vocabulary

Stillness, water, light, stone, breath, ritual, presence, warmth, renewal, quiet, touch, time, reflection, return.

## Avoid

Best, ultimate, revolutionary, amazing, five-star, number one, luxury experience, limited offer, instant results.
