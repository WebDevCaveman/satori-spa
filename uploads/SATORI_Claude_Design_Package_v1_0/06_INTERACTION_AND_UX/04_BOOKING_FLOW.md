# Booking Flow

## Steps

1. Choose sanctuary or location.
2. Choose ritual category.
3. Choose ritual.
4. Choose date and time.
5. Add guest preferences.
6. Review transparent price.
7. Confirm.

## Rules

- one meaningful decision per step,
- back navigation preserves data,
- clear progress,
- no forced account creation before availability,
- no scarcity pressure,
- no hidden price,
- no aggressive upsell,
- accessible labels and errors,
- mobile-friendly controls.

## Confirmation

Your time has been reserved.

We look forward to welcoming you into a slower rhythm.
