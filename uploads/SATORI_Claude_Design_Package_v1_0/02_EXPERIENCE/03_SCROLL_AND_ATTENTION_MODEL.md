# Scroll and Attention Model

## Core sequence

Reveal → Observe → Breathe → Continue

## Attention rules

- One primary movement at a time.
- One secondary environmental motion at most.
- When text enters, environmental motion decreases.
- After an emotional peak, create recovery.
- Do not reveal important copy while the scene is changing rapidly.
- Avoid continuous animation.
- Do not hijack scroll unnecessarily.
- The user must remain able to navigate and understand the page without scroll effects.

## Section transitions

Use:

- light change,
- foreground occlusion,
- reflection,
- mist,
- shadow,
- image crop,
- slow masks,
- and architectural compression/release.

Avoid:

- abrupt slides,
- card swipes,
- spinning,
- zoom tunnels,
- and excessive pinned sections.
