# Emotional Journey

## 1. Outside Noise
The visitor arrives distracted. Do not demand attention.

## 2. Pause
Darkness, water, mist, and waiting establish a break from ordinary browsing.

## 3. Curiosity
Reveal fragments before complete architecture.

## 4. Safety
Use warmth, clarity, and calm behavior. Avoid urgency.

## 5. Presence
Increase negative space and reduce motion.

## 6. Trust
Introduce philosophy, human care, and ritual precision.

## 7. Sensory Immersion
Show touch, water, steam, materials, breath, and architecture.

## 8. Desire
Let the visitor imagine being present. Do not aggressively persuade.

## 9. Renewal
Show the journey’s emotional transformation.

## 10. Reservation
Make the next step simple, transparent, and calm.

## 11. Memory
End with visual and emotional stillness.
