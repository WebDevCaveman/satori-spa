# Cinematic Media Direction

## Camera personality

Patient, quiet, respectful, observant.

## Preferred lenses

- 35 mm architecture and movement,
- 50 mm human perspective,
- 85 mm intimacy,
- 100 mm macro detail.

## Movement

- slow dolly,
- restrained lateral reveal,
- static observation,
- water-level movement,
- controlled push-in.

## Avoid

- dramatic drone reveals,
- fast orbit,
- handheld instability,
- impossible fly-throughs,
- constant rack focus,
- excessive slow motion.

## Image quality

Realistic materials, natural skin, restrained grade, low saturation, plausible architecture and light.
