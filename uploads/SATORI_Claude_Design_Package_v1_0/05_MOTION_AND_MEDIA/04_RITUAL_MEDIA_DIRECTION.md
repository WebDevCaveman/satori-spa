# Ritual Media Direction

Show:
- oil poured into ceramic,
- towel folded,
- basalt stone prepared,
- water touched,
- therapist hands,
- guest breath,
- tea poured after treatment.

Use:
- 85 mm,
- 100 mm macro,
- slow push-in,
- natural focus,
- warm side light.

Avoid:
- explicit beauty advertising,
- artificial smiles,
- clinical massage imagery,
- overly sensual framing,
- repetitive hand closeups without narrative progression.
