# Motion Rules

1. One primary motion at a time.
2. One secondary environmental motion at most.
3. Text and major environmental movement do not compete.
4. Emotional peaks require recovery.
5. Motion must reveal, guide, confirm, or connect.
6. Reduced-motion states are designed, not merely disabled.
7. Never use bounce or elastic effects.
8. No flying cards.
9. No decorative cursor trail.
10. No dramatic 3D rotation.
11. No auto-playing carousels.
12. Scroll behavior must remain understandable and controllable.
