# Human Presence Direction

## Guests

- relaxed posture,
- natural breathing,
- no camera awareness,
- slow movement,
- authentic age and body diversity,
- simple natural garments.

## Therapists

- precise,
- respectful,
- confident,
- quiet,
- presented as artisans.

## Gestures

- touching water,
- adjusting linen,
- pouring tea,
- opening timber screen,
- folding towel,
- resting hand on stone.

## Avoid

- influencer behavior,
- fashion posing,
- forced smiling,
- staged romance,
- culturally theatrical costumes.
