# Sound Direction

## Optional sound layer

Sound must always be opt-in or clearly controllable.

## Palette

- water drop,
- low water ambience,
- bamboo movement,
- fabric,
- tea pouring,
- breath,
- subtle fire,
- sparse tonal resonance.

## Avoid

- continuous spa music,
- loud ambient loops,
- autoplay without control,
- synthetic chimes,
- cinematic trailer sound.

## Accessibility

The experience must remain complete with sound off.
