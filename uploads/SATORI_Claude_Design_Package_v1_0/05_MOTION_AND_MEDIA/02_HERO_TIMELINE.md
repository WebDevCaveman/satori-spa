# Hero Timeline

## Scroll or time sequence

0–10%:
- darkness,
- isolated water sound.

10–25%:
- reflection appears,
- mist becomes visible.

25–45%:
- camera begins moving,
- water and stone establish depth.

45–65%:
- architecture enters.

65–80%:
- human presence enters.

80–100%:
- wordmark,
- headline,
- supporting line,
- delayed entry cue.

## Rule

Typography appears only after the scene has stabilized.
