# Design Principles

1. Every element must earn its place.
2. One viewport should communicate one primary idea.
3. Remove before adding.
4. Movement must reveal, guide, or confirm.
5. Whitespace is an architectural material.
6. Interface hierarchy remains below emotion, architecture, light, and story.
7. Typography supports the scene; it does not become the scene.
8. A section must remain meaningful with motion disabled.
9. Luxury is demonstrated through precision and restraint.
10. Booking is the conclusion of trust, not a permanent interruption.
11. Technology must remain invisible.
12. The project must not look like an AI-generated luxury template.
