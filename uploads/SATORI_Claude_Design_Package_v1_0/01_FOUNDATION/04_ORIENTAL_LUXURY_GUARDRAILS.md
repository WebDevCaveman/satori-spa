# Oriental Luxury Guardrails

## Express oriental influence through

- spatial restraint,
- framed views,
- shadow,
- water,
- timber,
- stone,
- ritual,
- hospitality choreography,
- gardens,
- handcraft,
- quiet asymmetry,
- and indoor-outdoor continuity.

## Do not use

- decorative dragons,
- generic calligraphy,
- fans as motifs,
- excessive red and gold,
- imitation temple ornaments,
- mixed Asian visual clichés,
- costume styling,
- mystical claims,
- generic “Zen” slogans,
- or ornamental patterns without cultural and spatial purpose.

## Test

Ask:

“Would this still feel oriental if all symbolic decorations were removed?”

If no, the concept is superficial and must be redesigned.
