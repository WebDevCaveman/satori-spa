# SCENE 02 — ARRIVAL & PHILOSOPHY

**Status:** Approved for production  
**Emotional role:** Curiosity → Safety → Presence

## Goal

Transition from cinematic intrigue into brand meaning without breaking immersion.

## Content

### Heading

**The art of returning.**

### Body

Leave the noise outside. Enter a slower rhythm shaped by water, natural light, and deliberate care.

### Supporting Line

SATORI is not a treatment destination. It is a return to presence.

## Visual Direction

- arrival court
- shallow reflecting pool
- one mature Japanese maple
- hidden reception
- warm cedar wall
- soft morning shadow
- no visible crowd
- no reception desk dominating the composition

## Camera

- mostly static or nearly static
- one slow lateral reveal through foreground bamboo
- architectural framing before human presence

## Interaction

- text revealed line by line
- background remains nearly still
- subtle reflection distortion tied to scroll velocity
- no cards
- no service icons

## Implementation

- pinned section, 120–160 vh
- typography enters only after scene stabilizes
- text max width: editorial, narrow
- no more than 45 words visible at once
