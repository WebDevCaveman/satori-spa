## Cinematic Sequence

The sequence moves from intimate to expansive:

1. narrow stone corridor
2. warm timber threshold
3. reflection hall
4. indoor-outdoor water sanctuary
5. forest path
6. infinity horizon

## Higgsfield MCP Prompt

Create a cinematic architectural sequence for SATORI, a fictional ultra-luxury oriental spa sanctuary. Begin in a narrow warm stone corridor with filtered daylight, move through a hinoki threshold, reveal a vast reflection hall with still water and soft steam, then transition into an indoor-outdoor thermal sanctuary surrounded by bamboo and moss, followed by a secluded forest path and finally an infinity pool aligned with a pale horizon. Contemporary Japanese minimalism, travertine, black basalt, cedar, frameless glass, water, mist, soft linen, subtle human presence. Camera movement is slow and respectful, 35 mm architecture lens, long controlled dolly, no impossible fly-throughs, no aggressive drone reveal, realistic light and material behavior, restrained palette, 1080p.

## Spatial Presentation

Avoid a gallery grid.

Use:

- one space per viewport
- architectural name
- one sentence
- minimal navigation indicator
- subtle progression through time of day

## Motion

- compression → release
- corridor sections use closer framing
- water sanctuary opens to full viewport
- infinity scene is the visual climax
- no larger reveal follows

## Implementation

- scroll-linked image sequence or scene crossfades
- scene transitions should be optical, not UI-like
- use masks, light, reflection, and foreground occlusion
- avoid obvious page-section boundaries
