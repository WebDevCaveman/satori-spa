# Architecture and Materials

## Heading direction

Architecture should be presented as a living system of light, shadow, water, and aging materials.

## Narrative topics

- travertine from dawn to lantern light,
- hinoki warmth and scent,
- basalt becoming reflective when wet,
- handmade ceramic objects,
- shadow as privacy,
- water as architecture,
- spaces designed to age beautifully.

## Presentation

Use immersive detail imagery and short editorial captions.

Do not create:
- e-commerce swatches,
- specification cards,
- generic material grids,
- or decorative samples without spatial context.
