# Guest Reflections

## Title

Reflections from SATORI.

## Format

Short editorial quotations with:
- name,
- city or location,
- optional discreet portrait.

## Rules

- no star ratings,
- no review logos,
- no testimonial carousel requiring auto-play,
- no rounded quote cards,
- no exaggerated claims.

## Tone

Specific, intimate, reflective.

Example:
“For the first time in months, I stopped thinking about time.”
