# SCENE 05 — NIGHT SANCTUARY & BOOKING

**Status:** Approved for production  
**Emotional role:** Reflection → Commitment → Memory

## Goal

Conclude the experience quietly and make booking feel like the natural next step.

## Visual Sequence

- blue hour settles over water
- lanterns appear one by one
- warm fire bowls
- dark reflective pool
- fabric moves in evening air
- guest rests near water
- booking interface appears within the scene
- final reflection remains after confirmation

## Higgsfield MCP Prompt

Create a slow cinematic night sanctuary sequence for SATORI Oriental Spa. Blue hour gradually becomes night. Warm lanterns illuminate stone paths, dark water reflects soft amber light, steam rises into cool evening air, fire bowls flicker gently, bamboo and linen move with a light breeze, and one guest rests quietly near the pool. The mood is private, safe, timeless, and emotionally warm. No nightlife aesthetic, no excessive darkness, no blue neon, no dramatic fantasy lighting. Use restrained 50 mm and 85 mm compositions, slow static-to-dolly movement, deep natural shadows, realistic reflections, quiet luxury, 1080p.

## Booking Copy

### Heading

**Reserve your moment of stillness.**

### Supporting Text

Choose a ritual, preferred sanctuary, and time. We will guide the rest.

### Primary CTA

**Begin your reservation**

### Secondary CTA

**Speak with a host**

## Booking Flow

1. Choose location or sanctuary
2. Choose ritual category
3. Choose date and time
4. Add guest preferences
5. Review
6. Confirm

## Booking Rules

- no urgency language
- no countdowns
- no “only two left”
- no aggressive upsell
- progressive disclosure
- one decision per step
- calm error handling
- clear pricing before confirmation

## Motion

- all environmental motion reduces when booking begins
- booking panel enters as an architectural layer, not a modal
- confirmation ends with a still reflection and short message

## Confirmation Copy

**Your time has been reserved.**

We look forward to welcoming you into a slower rhythm.

## Implementation

- accessible multi-step form
- save progress locally
- full keyboard support
- reduced motion mode
- no blocking background video on mobile
