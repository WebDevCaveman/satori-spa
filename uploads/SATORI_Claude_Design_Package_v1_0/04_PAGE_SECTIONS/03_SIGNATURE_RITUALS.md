Introduce services through ritual, touch, and craft rather than a conventional service grid.

## Cinematic Sequence

- macro shot of oil poured into a ceramic bowl
- warm towel folded with precision
- therapist prepares stone and linen
- hands touch water
- treatment begins
- steam drifts through warm side light
- guest exhales
- tea is poured at the close

## Higgsfield MCP Prompt

Create a slow, intimate cinematic wellness ritual for SATORI Oriental Spa. Focus on craftsmanship and human touch: a skilled therapist preparing natural oils in handmade ceramic vessels, folding warm linen towels, placing smooth basalt stones, pouring water, and beginning a precise restorative massage. Show hands, breath, fabric, steam, skin, natural oils, warm stone, and hinoki wood. No exaggerated smiles, no beauty-advertising poses, no clinical environment. Camera language is restrained and intimate: 85 mm portraits, 100 mm macro details, slow push-ins, minimal focus shifts, long takes, natural skin texture, candle and warm side light, soft shadows, quiet luxury, realistic human gestures, 1080p.

## UI Pattern

Use a vertical ritual index rather than cards.

Each ritual reveals:

- name
- one-line emotional promise
- duration
- optional “Discover the ritual”
