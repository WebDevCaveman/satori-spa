# SCENE 01 — HERO

**Status:** Approved for production  
**Emotional role:** Arrival → Pause → Curiosity

## Goal

Slow the visitor within the first 8–12 seconds and establish SATORI as a place of stillness, ritual, and architectural calm.

## Visual Sequence

1. Black screen.
2. One distant water drop.
3. Reflection appears before architecture.
4. Morning mist becomes visible.
5. Camera begins an extremely slow glide over water.
6. Travertine edge enters frame.
7. Bamboo shadow moves softly.
8. A guest in an ivory robe crosses a stone path.
9. SATORI wordmark appears as light reveals it.

## Environment

- dawn
- reflection pool
- Japanese-inspired contemporary architecture
- warm travertine
- hinoki wood
- bamboo
- soft steam
- pale sky
- no visible city or commercial signage

## Human Direction

- one adult guest
- natural posture
- no direct camera engagement
- slow barefoot walk
- robe moves naturally with air
- no theatrical expression

## Camera

- lens: 35–50 mm
- height: water level rising gradually to eye level
- movement: very slow forward dolly
- rotation: almost none
- stabilization: perfect
- no drone-style reveal
- no dramatic orbit

## Lighting

- cool dawn ambient
- first warm sunlight touching stone
- low contrast
- volumetric light only where steam makes it physically plausible

## Higgsfield MCP Prompt

Create an ultra-premium cinematic hero sequence for SATORI, a fictional contemporary oriental spa. Dawn in a secluded architectural sanctuary. The camera glides extremely slowly at water level across a perfectly still reflection pool. Pale morning mist drifts above the surface. Warm natural travertine, hinoki wood, bamboo shadows, frameless glass, and restrained Japanese-inspired architecture gradually emerge. A calm adult guest in an ivory linen spa robe walks barefoot across a floating stone path, never posing or looking at camera. Soft sunrise begins to touch the stone and steam. Natural movement only. 35–50 mm cinematic lens language, long continuous take, subtle depth of field, elegant negative space, realistic water reflections, restrained color grading, no oversaturation, no fantasy effects, no stock-photo feeling, no dramatic camera moves. Slow luxury, quiet confidence, Awwwards-level art direction, 1080p.
