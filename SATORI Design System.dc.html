Usage
Environmental color carries the scene; interface controls stay neutral (Charcoal on Ivory, Ivory on Dark Water Blue). Bronze appears only as a thin acknowledgment — rules, focus, small marks. Amber is reserved for the night chapter.
Never
No saturated green, gold, orange, or blue. No accent covering large areas. No beige-and-orange AI luxury gradient. Text contrast verified on every ground.

Two families only: Cormorant Garamond for headlines, ritual names, and short quotations; Work Sans for navigation, body, labels, forms, and accessibility text. No ultra-thin weights for essential copy. Long text is never centered. Not every heading is oversized — scale follows emotional weight.

Labels always visible above the field. One decision per step. Validation speaks quietly, in full sentences, never in red alarm tones — error uses a muted clay, success uses moss.
07

Image Containers

[ cinematic 21:9 — still water, filtered light, one dominant focus ]
[ 3:2 — architectural frame, negative space ]
[ 4:5 — human gesture, restrained depth ]
No frames, no radius, no drop shadows. Images sit directly on the ground or bleed with purpose. Real media replaces these placeholders in later phases — no AI-render perfection, no stock smiles.
